\section{Formalization and Mechanization}
\label{sec:formalization}

To establish the mathematical soundness of our spatiotemporal capability framework, we mechanized its operational semantics, Kripke world structure, and logical relation in Rocq/Coq.

\subsection{Spatiotemporal Kripke Frame and Capability Decay}

A Kripke world $w = (\Lambda, m, n, \nu)$ models the execution environment, where $\Lambda$ denotes the active capability authority set, $m$ is the reference monitor configuration state, $n \in \mathbb{N}$ represents step-indexed metric operational fuel, and $\nu \in \mathbb{N}$ represent the temporal epoch. World accessibility $w \sqsubseteq w'$ (defined as \texttt{world\_accessible} in our development) governs valid temporal and spatial evolution:
\[
(\Lambda, m, n, \nu) \sqsubseteq (\Lambda', m', n', \nu') \iff \Lambda' \subseteq \Lambda \land m \le m' \land n' \le n \land \nu \le \nu'
\]
This relation captures fundamental domain constraints: **spatial contraction** (authority can only be withdrawn or restricted, $\Lambda' \subseteq \Lambda$), **temporal advancement** (epochs monotonically increase, $\nu \le \nu'$), and **metric metric decay** (fuel strictly decays to ensure termination over steps, $n' < n$ under reduction steps).

\paragraph{Contravariant Capability Decay.}
Capability validity, $\mathtt{valid\_cap}(c, w) \iff c \in \Lambda \land \nu \le \text{cap\_max\_epoch}(c)$, is intentionally non-monotone across forward world evolution ($w \sqsubseteq w'$). Because $w \sqsubseteq w'$ allows spatial revocation ($c \notin \Lambda'$) and epoch expiration ($\nu' > \text{cap\_max\_epoch}(c)$), forward validity preservation ($\mathtt{valid\_cap}(c, w) \implies \mathtt{valid\_cap}(c, w')$) is false by design. Within our mechanization, this contravariant decay is isolated to an explicit domain parameter:
\begin{lstlisting}[language=Coq]
Axiom valid_cap_decay_axiom : forall (c : Capability) (w w' : World),
  world_accessible w w' -> valid_cap c w' -> valid_cap c w.
\end{lstlisting}
This backward preservation axiom reflects the physics of spatiotemporal frames: if a capability token is valid in a future world $w'$, it was necessarily valid in the earlier world $w$.

\subsection{Dual-Path Logical Relation and Operational Trapping}

The value interpretation $\mathcal{V}_w \llbracket T \rrbracket$ handles capability expiration gracefully through a dual-path operational boundary:
\[
\mathcal{V}_w \llbracket \mathtt{TCap} \rrbracket (v) \triangleq \big(v = \mathtt{e\_invoke}~c \land \mathtt{valid\_cap}(c, w)\big) \;\;\lor\;\; v = \mathtt{e\_val}~0
\]
When a world transition $w \sqsubseteq w'$ causes a capability token $c$ to expire or undergo revocation ($\neg \mathtt{valid\_cap}(c, w')$), the term $v$ drops into the safe recovery branch ($v = \mathtt{e\_val}~0$). At runtime, the monitored step relation $\mathtt{step\_m}$ intercepts invalidated invocations and transitions them safely via an operational trap ($\mathtt{eff\_trap}$). This keeps term interpretation monotone without forcing individual capability tokens to persist indefinitely.
